Annate Bitherapeutics is proud to share that the company won at the **2026 GIPA Startup Awards**, a Georgia Intellectual Property Alliance event recognizing outstanding Georgia startups that leverage intellectual property as a strategic business asset. <!--more-->

The awards ceremony was held on **May 20, 2026**, in Atlanta. GIPA describes the Startup Awards as a program recognizing and financially supporting early-stage Georgia companies that demonstrate innovation and strategic use of intellectual property, with award recipients gaining public recognition and access to GIPA’s mentorship, investor, industry, and policymaker network.

In a LinkedIn post following the event, Annate CEO **Eric DeJesus, PhD**, thanked the Georgia Intellectual Property Alliance for recognizing startups using intellectual property to build meaningful, high-impact companies and noted Annate’s continued work advancing its ApoL1-based bispecific antibody platform for multiple myeloma therapy.
